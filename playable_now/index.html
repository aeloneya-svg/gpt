EchoRift — 3rd Person 3D

Full 3rd-person camera, proper WASD, click-to-shoot guns, stable Rift events, and a desert combat world.

Controls

  • Move: WASD
  • Aim/Turn: Mouse (click game to lock cursor)
  • Shoot: Left click
  • Jump: Space
  • Build cover in front: B
  • Switch weapon: 1 / 2
  • Pause: P
  • Restart: Enter
